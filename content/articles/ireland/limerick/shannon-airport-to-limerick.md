Getting from Shannon Airport to Limerick is much simpler than the distance between an airport and another city might suggest.

For most independent travellers, the first option to look at is Bus Éireann Route 343. It connects Shannon Airport with Limerick, runs throughout the day and night, and removes the need to arrange a rental car or private pickup just to reach the city.

Taxi and private transfer are still useful alternatives, especially when time or luggage matters more than price.

This guide combines firsthand local input with current information from Bus Éireann and Transport for Ireland.

If the rest of your trip will also be car-free, see our [Limerick Without a Car guide](/ireland/limerick/limerick-without-a-car/).

## Shannon Airport to Limerick at a glance

For most travellers, the choices are straightforward.

| Option | Best for | Main advantage | Main drawback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Route 343 bus | Most solo travellers, couples and budget-conscious visitors | Direct, frequent and available through the night | Makes intermediate stops and can be affected by traffic or service disruption |
| Taxi | Travellers in a hurry or carrying a lot of luggage | Direct door-to-door journey | Considerably more expensive than the bus |
| Private transfer | Travellers who want a pre-arranged pickup | Convenience and a planned door-to-door service | Usually the most expensive option |

For a normal arrival with manageable luggage and accommodation in or near central Limerick, Route 343 is the option I would consider first from an editorial point of view.

## Bus 343 is the best option for most travellers

Bus Éireann Route 343 connects Limerick and Shannon, including Shannon Airport.

Transport for Ireland introduced the route as a 24-hour service, making it unusually useful for an airport connection outside Dublin.

Official service information has included departures as frequently as every 20 minutes during weekday peak periods and approximately every 30 minutes during much of the rest of the weekday schedule.

Night services operate at a lower frequency.

Do not plan around those frequency figures alone. Timetables can change, individual departures can be cancelled or diverted, and traffic can affect arrival times.

Check the current Route 343 timetable and TFI Live after landing.

For the journey into central Limerick, it is sensible to allow roughly an hour rather than building a tight onward connection around a perfect timetable.

## Where do you catch the 343 at Shannon Airport?

One of the best things about this route is that finding it does not require a complicated airport transfer.

After leaving the arrivals area, head outside the terminal and follow the bus and coach signs.

Based on firsthand local input, the stop is immediately outside the terminal and was very clearly marked.

The Route 343 number and Limerick destination were also easy to identify.

That matters after a flight. You should not need to navigate to another terminal, take an airport shuttle or walk to a distant transport interchange before beginning the journey to Limerick.

## Do you need to book the bus in advance?

For the normal Route 343 journey, arriving and paying for the trip can be simple.

On the firsthand journey used for this guide, the traveller arrived at the stop, boarded the bus without a reservation and paid the driver in cash.

There was no complicated ticket collection process.

Bus Éireann also supports TFI Leap Card and eligible tickets through the TFI Go app.

If paying cash, Transport for Ireland advises passengers to have the exact change when possible.

Do not assume that tapping a normal bank card on the driver's machine works in the same way as contactless transport in some other countries. Check the current Bus Éireann payment information before depending on a particular payment method.

## What is the 343 like with luggage?

![Generic editorial illustration of airport bus travel with luggage](/images/limerick/shannon-airport-to-limerick/airport-bus-luggage-illustration.webp)

The bus is a realistic airport option even if you have a suitcase.

The vehicle used on the firsthand trip had dedicated luggage space, so the traveller did not need to keep a large bag at the seat.

That makes the bus practical for a normal airport arrival.

Vehicle layouts can vary, however, so travellers with several large suitcases, mobility requirements or awkward equipment may still find a taxi or transfer more convenient.

The practical dividing line is simple: normal luggage does not automatically justify paying for a private car. A large amount of luggage might.

## What is it like inside the bus?

The exact vehicle can change, so avoid assuming every Route 343 bus has identical equipment.

On the bus used for the firsthand trip, there were screens showing the upcoming stops and estimated times.

The passenger information appeared in both English and Irish.

There were also USB ports for charging a phone.

That is particularly useful after a flight because keeping a phone charged matters if you are using Google Maps, booking accommodation transport or following your route through Limerick.

The traveller does not remember whether Wi-Fi was available, so this guide does not assume that it is.

## How do you know where to get off?

You do not necessarily need to remain on the bus until Colbert Station.

The Route 343 serves Limerick before reaching or terminating around the city's main transport hub, depending on the current service pattern.

If your accommodation is in central Limerick, an earlier city stop may be more convenient.

On the firsthand trip, Google Maps was used to follow the journey and choose the central stop.

The bus's onboard passenger information also made the progression of stops easy to follow.

Check your accommodation location before arriving in Limerick and compare it with the current Route 343 stops.

If you are connecting to a train or another bus, continuing to Colbert Station may make more sense.

![Limerick Colbert Station in Limerick, Ireland](/images/limerick/shannon-airport-to-limerick/limerick-colbert-station.webp)

Colbert is Limerick's main rail and bus station and is the most logical arrival point when your next journey begins there.

If your hotel or accommodation is closer to the city centre, however, there is no benefit in automatically staying on the bus longer than necessary.

## A useful detail about getting off the bus

There is no reason to panic and stand beside the door long before your stop.

On the firsthand journey, the traveller pressed the stop button while still seated and waited until the bus had actually stopped before getting up.

The driver gave passengers time to get off before continuing.

Treat that as an observation from this particular journey rather than a formal operating guarantee.

The useful lesson is simply to watch the onboard information or Google Maps, signal for the stop in good time and avoid trying to balance beside the exit unnecessarily while the bus is moving.

## Is the 343 practical if you arrive at night?

Yes, and this is one of the route's biggest advantages.

Route 343 operates through the night, although frequency is lower than during the busiest daytime periods.

The firsthand arrival used for this guide took place at night.

The bus stop outside Shannon Airport felt safe, comfortable and straightforward to use. The wait happened to be short and the bus itself was almost empty.

Those last two details were specific to that journey and should not be expected every night.

The more important point is that a late arrival does not automatically force you into paying for a taxi.

Check TFI Live after landing, confirm the next departure and decide from there.

For someone staying centrally in Limerick, arriving by bus and then walking to accommodation can also be practical. That is what happened on the firsthand journey used for this guide.

## How reliable is the Shannon Airport to Limerick bus?

The 343 is a regular public transport service rather than an occasional tourist shuttle.

That makes it a strong default option, but no bus service should be treated as immune to delays or cancellations.

Traffic, roadworks, operational problems and temporary diversions can affect individual departures.

This matters most if you are working with a strict connection.

After landing, check the current departure rather than relying only on a screenshot of a timetable saved days earlier.

If the next bus is operating normally and you are not in a hurry, there is little reason to overcomplicate the journey.

## Taxi from Shannon Airport to Limerick

A taxi makes most sense when convenience matters more than keeping the transfer inexpensive.

The road journey is direct, and a taxi removes the need to choose a bus stop or walk from the city centre with luggage.

It is particularly useful if:

- you are in a hurry
- you have several heavy bags
- mobility makes handling luggage on a bus difficult
- your accommodation is outside the central area
- several people are sharing the fare

Taxi fares in Ireland are regulated by the National Transport Authority and calculated using the taximeter.

The final price depends on the journey, traffic, time of travel and applicable extras.

Pre-booking can also add an official booking fee.

For that reason, this article should not pretend that there is one guaranteed Shannon Airport to Limerick taxi price.

Use the current TFI Taxi Fare Estimator when you want an approximate figure.

That can make sense after a long flight, with substantial luggage, for a group, or when reaching the destination quickly matters more than saving money.

## Bus, taxi or transfer: which should you choose?

Choose the 343 if you are travelling alone or as a couple, have manageable luggage and are heading to central Limerick.

Choose a taxi if you need to leave the airport quickly, have a lot of luggage or want to travel directly to an address without thinking about stops.

Choose a private transfer if you prefer having the journey arranged before arrival and are willing to pay more for that convenience.

For most normal airport arrivals, the bus is not merely the cheapest compromise. It is a genuinely practical way to reach Limerick.

The fact that it operates through the night makes that recommendation stronger.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Shannon Airport to Limerick?

Yes. Bus Éireann Route 343 directly connects Shannon Airport and Limerick.

### Does the Shannon Airport to Limerick bus run at night?

Yes. Route 343 is a 24-hour service, although nighttime departures are less frequent than the busiest daytime services. Always check the current timetable and live departures.

### Can I pay cash on the 343?

Yes. Bus Éireann accepts cash on board its public services. TFI advises passengers paying cash to have exact change when possible. Leap Card and eligible TFI Go tickets are also available payment options.

### Can I pay the driver with a normal contactless bank card?

Do not depend on this without checking current Bus Éireann payment information. Official payment information distinguishes between cash or transport products used on board and card payments available at ticket vending machines.

### Do I need to reserve Route 343 before landing?

For a normal journey, you can board and pay for the service without building the trip around a reserved seat. The firsthand traveller used for this guide simply arrived, boarded and paid cash.

### Is there room for luggage on the bus?

The vehicle used on the firsthand journey had dedicated luggage space. Vehicle layout can vary, so passengers with unusually large amounts of luggage should check current accessibility or luggage information if it matters to the trip.

### Where should I get off in Limerick?

It depends on where you are staying. A central stop may be more convenient than continuing to Colbert Station. Use the current Route 343 stop list together with your accommodation location. Continue to Colbert if you need the main train or bus station.

### Is the bus safe for a nighttime arrival?

The nighttime airport stop and bus felt safe and comfortable during the firsthand journey used for this guide. That is an individual experience, not a guarantee of conditions at every time. Route 343's 24-hour operation nevertheless makes it a practical option for late arrivals.

### Is taxi better than the bus?

Usually not for a traveller going to central Limerick with normal luggage. Taxi becomes more attractive when you are in a hurry, carrying a lot of luggage or need a direct journey to somewhere inconvenient from the bus route.
