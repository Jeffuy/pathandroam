Yes, you can travel from Limerick to the Cliffs of Moher without a car. The useful public transport route starts at Limerick Colbert Station, changes in Ennis and finishes almost at the entrance to the Cliffs of Moher Experience.

It is a long day, with roughly 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes of travel each way, but the journey is straightforward when the connection works. The bus is particularly attractive for travellers who care more about cost than flexibility.

For wider transport planning in the city, start with our [Limerick Without a Car guide](/ireland/limerick/limerick-without-a-car/).

## Limerick to the Cliffs of Moher at a glance

> **The short answer**
>
> - **Is it possible?** Yes, by changing buses in Ennis.
> - **Journey time:** Allow roughly 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes each way.
> - **Best for:** Budget-conscious travellers staying in Limerick.
> - **Main advantage:** Public transport can cost much less than an organised tour.
> - **Main drawback:** The day depends on fixed connections, especially the return Route 350.

## Can you visit the Cliffs of Moher from Limerick without a car?

The trip is realistic as a day out, but there is no direct public bus from Limerick to the Cliffs. The cleanest route to check is:

**Limerick Colbert Station → Route 51 to Ennis → Route 350 to the Cliffs of Moher.**

Bus Éireann currently lists Expressway Route 51 as a Cork to Galway service that calls at Limerick, Shannon Airport and Ennis. Route 350 officially runs between Ennis and Galway via the Cliffs of Moher.

The exact connection changes with the timetable, so check both route pages and the TFI journey planner for your travel date. Do not build the day around the sample times below without verifying them again.

The return bus matters most. Missing the planned Route 350 from the Cliffs can leave you with a long wait or no useful connection back to Limerick that evening.

## Prefer an easier day trip?

If you want to skip the Ennis change, avoid watching the clock for the return bus or prefer a simpler day out, an organised tour from Limerick can be easier.

## The best public transport route from Limerick

![Limerick Colbert Station in Limerick, Ireland](/images/limerick/shannon-airport-to-limerick/limerick-colbert-station.webp)

### Limerick Colbert to Ennis on Route 51

Start at Limerick Colbert Station and look for the Route 51 service towards Galway. On one weekday trip, the coach was comfortable and easy to board at Colbert, which was the starting point for the day.

The first leg took about one hour. Route 51 remains the direct service to check for this part of the journey, but confirm the current departure and any service updates before leaving.

### Changing buses in Ennis

The connection in Ennis took around 15 to 20 minutes on that trip. It felt well coordinated rather than like a long wait.

Once you get off Route 51, go directly to the Route 350 bay. Several passengers made the same connection that morning, and joining the queue quickly was more useful than trying to fit in a coffee.

![Illustrated traveller waiting beside a coach at a bus station](/images/articles/limerick-to-cliffs-of-moher-without-a-car/public-bus-connection-illustration.png)

### Ennis to the Cliffs on Route 350

Route 350 continues through County Clare and stops at the Cliffs of Moher. On the weekday journey described here, this leg took about 1 hour 5 minutes.

The stop is beside the main parking area, across from the Cliffs of Moher Experience. The entrance is roughly a two-minute walk away, so there is no additional rural transfer at the end.

## Example day trip timetable

This is one worked example from a spring Wednesday, not a timetable for every day.

| Stage | Example time |
| --- | --- |
| Leave Limerick Colbert on Route 51 | 08:25 |
| Arrive in Ennis | 09:25 |
| Leave Ennis on Route 350 | 09:45 |
| Arrive at the Cliffs of Moher | Around 10:50 |
| Leave the Cliffs on Route 350 | 15:20 |
| Arrive in Ennis | Around 16:30 |
| Return to Limerick Colbert | Around 17:45 |

This example left roughly four and a half hours between arrival and the return bus. Around three hours was enough to cover the main viewpoints, visitor centre, O'Brien's Tower area, a short permitted walk and a quick café and shop stop without rushing. The time available depends on the current timetable.

Arrive at the return stop 10 to 15 minutes early. The outbound connection is important, but the last useful Route 350 back to Ennis is the part of the day that deserves the closest attention.

## How much does the public bus cost?

On that spring trip, the return journey cost roughly €12 to €15 with a TFI Leap Card. That is an approximate account of one trip, not a guaranteed current fare.

The Leap Card was tapped when boarding each leg after giving the destination. The two buses were charged as separate journeys rather than one combined Limerick to Cliffs ticket.

Transport for Ireland says a Leap Card can save up to 30% on adult cash fares and can be used on Bus Éireann services. Check the current fare and payment information for both routes before travel, particularly if you plan to use TFI Go instead of a physical Leap Card.

The cost advantage can be substantial compared with a tour, but the trade-off is a longer day governed by public bus times.

## What is the bus journey like?

Allow roughly 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes in each direction, including the Ennis connection.

Boarding Route 51 at Colbert offered a comfortable start to the day. Route 350 was also manageable, although the connection attracted a noticeable group of passengers and it was sensible to join the queue quickly.

The journey through County Clare is part of the day rather than dead time, but five hours on buses is still significant. Carry water and a small snack, keep the current timetables available on your phone and avoid planning anything time-sensitive for immediately after the return to Limerick.

## What can you do in about three hours at the Cliffs?

Three hours was enough to cover the main viewpoints, spend time around O'Brien's Tower and walk along the permitted paths within the visitor area.

The visit also included the exhibition inside the visitor centre, its immersive film presentation, a coffee and snack stop, and time in the shop. O'Brien's Tower can close for weather or operational reasons, so treat it as a possible part of the visit rather than a guarantee.

On a spring Wednesday, the site felt fairly calm overall. The busiest areas were around the visitor centre and O'Brien's Tower. The paths became noticeably quieter after walking a short distance away from the centre.

The weather changed quickly between cloud, drizzle and brief breaks of sun, with strong wind throughout much of the visit.

## Important 2026 coastal walk restrictions

![Illustrated traveller walking on a clifftop path above the Atlantic](/images/articles/limerick-to-cliffs-of-moher-without-a-car/coastal-cliff-path-illustration.png)

Do not plan this day trip around completing the full coastal walk to Doolin or Hags Head.

The official 2026 closure information says the southern coastal route between Liscannor or Hags Head and the Cliffs of Moher Experience is fully closed. On the northern side, walkers starting in Doolin must turn back at Surfers Path and cannot continue through to the Cliffs of Moher Experience.

Within the Cliffs of Moher Experience area, visitors can follow roughly 3.5 km or 2.17 miles of safe designated walking. The route goes north past O'Brien's Tower before returning the same way, then south to the Southern Viewing Platform. This does not reopen the through-routes to Doolin or Hags Head.

Follow onsite signs, barriers and staff guidance. Conditions and closures can change, so check the official coastal walk page shortly before travelling.

## What to bring for the day

- a waterproof jacket with a hood
- comfortable footwear with good grip
- layers for changing temperatures
- water and a small snack
- enough charge and mobile data to check live departures and maps

An umbrella is a poor substitute for a hooded jacket when the wind is strong. Even a mild forecast can feel colder on the exposed clifftop paths.

## Public bus or organised tour: which is better?

Choose the public bus if you are staying in Limerick, want to keep costs down and are comfortable managing one connection in each direction.

An organised tour may suit travellers with very limited time, families with small children, older travellers, or anyone who wants a simpler all-in-one day. A tour can also make more sense if you want to combine the Cliffs with other stops without coordinating several public services.

The bus gives you control while you are onsite, but only within the limits of the return timetable. A tour gives up some independence in exchange for simpler transport.

## Is the Cliffs of Moher day trip from Limerick worth doing by bus?

Yes, especially for a budget-conscious traveller who is prepared for a long day.

The route is easier than it looks on a map. Route 51 takes you from Limerick to Ennis, Route 350 completes the journey, and the final stop is almost at the entrance.

The two rules that matter are simple: go directly to the Route 350 queue when you reach Ennis, and arrive at the return stop at the Cliffs 10 to 15 minutes early.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct bus from Limerick to the Cliffs of Moher?

No. The useful public transport route changes in Ennis, usually from Route 51 to Route 350. Check the current journey planner before travelling.

### How long does the bus take from Limerick to the Cliffs of Moher?

Allow roughly 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes each way, including the connection in Ennis.

### How long should you spend at the Cliffs of Moher?

About three hours is enough for the main viewpoints, permitted paths and visitor centre at a comfortable pace. A longer visit is harder to arrange by public bus because the return timetable limits flexibility.

### Can you use a Leap Card for the journey?

A Leap Card was used on both legs of the example trip, with each journey charged separately. Transport for Ireland lists Bus Éireann among the participating services, but check current fare and acceptance details for the specific buses before travelling.

### Can you walk from the Cliffs of Moher Experience to Doolin?

Not under the current 2026 closure arrangements. The northern route from Doolin ends at Surfers Path, with no access through to the Cliffs of Moher Experience. Check the official closure page before planning any coastal walk.
